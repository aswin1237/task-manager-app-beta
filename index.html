📚 Core UX Laws & Usability Heuristics Audit

How OmniTask's architecture adheres to cognitive science & Nielsen Norman Group principles.

📐
Fitts's Law Distance & Target Size

"The time required to rapidly move to a target area is a function of the ratio between distance and width."

In OmniTask:
  • Primary "+ New Entry" button pinned in comfortable reach.
  • Senior mode enforces extra-large 56×56dp tactile touch targets to eliminate motor tremor errors.
  • Emergency 🚨 SOS button given maximum width and high-contrast red treatment.
🧠
Hick's Law Decision Time ($T = b \cdot \log_2(n+1)$)

"The time it takes to make a decision increases logarithmically with the number and complexity of choices."

In OmniTask:
  • Replaced a chaotic list of 15+ task types with 5 distinct top-level module categories (Pill, Payment, Appointment, Fitness, Habit).
  • Elderly interface reduces 4 navigation tabs into a single linear timeline of today's events.
🧭
Jakob's Law User Familiarity & Expectations

"Users spend most of their time on other apps. They prefer your app to work the same way as all the others they already know."

In OmniTask:
  • Retained standard Monday–Sunday 7-column calendar matrix.
  • Universal spotlight search triggers via universal desktop shortcut Ctrl + K.
  • Familiar bottom/sidebar navigation patterns with standard icon signifiers.
📦
Miller's Law Working Memory Capacity ($7 \pm 2$)

"The average person can only keep 7 (plus or minus 2) items in their active working memory."

In OmniTask:
  • Progressive disclosure in the "+ New Entry" flow: Step 1 (Choose module) $\rightarrow$ Step 2 (Fill details).
  • Same-day recurring habits chunked under emoji clusters (e.g. 🎸 ×2 Grouped).
✨
Aesthetic-Usability Effect Perception of Usability

"Users perceive aesthetically pleasing design as design that’s more usable and trustworthy."

In OmniTask:
  • Replaced stress-inducing red calendar cells with soothing Royal Violet for high task density.
  • Glassmorphic cards, polished dark-mode palette, and micro-animations inspire clinical trust.
🛡️
Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics Industry Gold Standard Evaluation

Evaluated against industry standards: Visibility of Status, User Freedom, Error Prevention, and Recognition.

In OmniTask:
  • Status Visibility: Real-time caregiver status dot + SLA response counter.
  • User Control & Freedom: Temporary Privacy Pause (2h/6h/24h) + Unilateral Caregiver Freeze.
  • Error Prevention: Master PIN verification (9412) prevents accidental senior abandonment.
