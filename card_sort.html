📂 The 6-Bucket Domain Model (Targeting Natural Granularity)

This 6-bucket model isolates distinct life domains into dedicated categories to test whether users prefer granular domain separation:

  • 💊 Health & Medicine: C-01 (Pill), C-02 (Medical ID), C-03 (Doctor Appt), C-16 (Emergency Buzz)
  • 💳 Bills & Money: C-04 (Utility Bill), C-05 (Rent), C-15 (Grocery Expense), C-21 (Spent Ledger)
  • 🏋️ Exercise & Fitness: C-12 (Gym Session), C-13 (AI Workout), C-14 (Weekly Goal)
  • ✨ Personal Habits: C-06 (Guitar), C-07 (Water Intake)
  • 📅 Calendar & Planning: C-08 (Month View), C-09 (Day Timeline), C-10 (Daily Spend)
  • 🛡️ Caregiver & Safety: C-17 (Caregiver Feed), C-18 (Privacy Pause), C-19 (Caregiver Freeze)

📱 The 4-Bucket Streamlined Model (Mobile Thumb-Zone Rule)

Apple HIG and Material Design 3 recommend 3 to 5 bottom tabs. The 4-bucket model consolidates Pills and Bills under "Reminders" ($ \text{Due Date} + \text{Mark Done} $) to keep the mobile bottom navigation uncluttered.

  • Model A (4-Tab Hub): Calendar | Reminders & Health | Fitness | Finances
  • Model B (6-Domain Hub): The 6 dedicated categories above.

🧠 The 3 Common Mental Models in Open Card Sorting

When participants do an Open Sort (with no pre-set categories), their groupings almost always reveal one of these three distinct psychological frameworks:

A

Pattern A: Domain-Specific Grouping (Common for Young Adults)

Users create 5 to 6 distinct topical silos: "Health & Meds", "Bills & Payments", "Exercise", "Habits", "Calendar", and "Caregiver Settings".

B

Pattern B: Urgency & Frequency Grouping (Common for Seniors)

Seniors group by routine frequency: "Things I Do Every Single Day" (Pills, Water), "Monthly Bills & Big Appointments" (Rent, Doctor), and "Emergency & Help" (SOS Buzz, Caregiver).

C

Pattern C: Role-Based Grouping (Common for Nurses & Guardians)

Healthcare professionals group by authority: "Patient Daily View" (Simple pills, Today feed), "Caregiver Telemetry" (Adherence feed, Medical ID), and "Personal Finances".
