📖 How Card Sorting Works & Participant Guide

Thank you for helping us design OmniTask! Card sorting is a proven UX research exercise that helps us understand how you naturally organize tasks, health reminders, and finances.

1

Read the 24 Feature Cards

On the left panel, you will see 24 cards representing everyday features (such as taking a prescription pill, paying an electricity bill, or logging a gym workout).

2

Group Related Items Together

Drag and drop cards into the category bucket where you would naturally expect to find them inside an app navigation menu. (On phones, use the dropdown below each card).

3

Rename or Create New Categories

If the existing categories don't fit your mental model, click "+ Add Custom Category" or click on any category title to rename it!

4

Export When Done

When all 24 cards are placed, click "💾 Export Data" to generate your study results!

🌟 Golden Rule: There are no right or wrong answers! We are testing our design, not you. Place things wherever makes the most sense to your daily routine.
